09What's Next

The Foundation is the heart. The retreat is the doorway. The platform is the future.

Version one was built for our retreat guests. Version two opens the work to the wider world. A public wellbeing platform, more retreat formats, a trained facilitator network, corporate programmes, scholarship places for people who could not otherwise access the work, and a long-term home for everyone who has walked through it. The same practice, scaled.

A new frontier

We are leaning into artificial intelligence as the next chapter of the work.

NAVID is live. EduAI is in build. The Foundation, with Divergence, is using AI to scale practice, teach the teachers, and reach the people we could not reach otherwise. Not as a replacement for the work. As a way to widen the door.

02

LHFF App

Mobile · iOS & Android

A native iOS and Android app for members and retreat graduates. Daily practice, curriculum library, NAVID, cohort messaging. Built so the work continues every day after the retreat ends.

03

EduAI

In development · Education

A platform we are building to empower young African teachers through AI. The idea is simple: train the teacher, the teacher trains the kids. Many of the libraries and schools we work with are running on outdated information, with limited connectivity. EduAI brings modern teaching tools, AI-assisted lesson planning, and Starlink-powered Wi-Fi into the same package.

Still early. Pilots planned with partner schools across Mozambique and South Africa.

12The Fashion Perspective

When we say fashion, we mean culture.

Not clothes. A whole culture that shapes how young people see themselves and the world they inherit. The Foundation uses fashion as a tool, never as a product. The shoots happen at Studio One. The story carries through the retreats, the curriculum, and the platform.

Fashion, the way we mean it.

01
A tool, not a product.

We use fashion to shape culture, not to sell it. The shoots, the styling, and the editorial all serve the mission. Revenue is not the metric.

02
Models as collaborators.

We work alongside young models from a human rights perspective. Fashion as education, culture, and work. Never exploitation. Protections in writing.

03
Made at Studio One.

Every shoot, film, and editorial is produced in-house at the Foundation's Cape Town studio. Same team, same standards, same care, every time.

In his own words

We are not using fashion to sell things. We are using it to move things. Starting with how young women see themselves.

Cheney Founder & CEO · Love Happiness Freedom Foundation
