The premise02 / 08

No gatekeeping. No fees. No agencies.

We're not looking
for the polished.
We're looking
for the real.

Studio One is built for young creatives the industry hasn't found yet. Photographers, models, designers, filmmakers, writers. If you have the eye, the voice, the instinct, you don't need a degree, a manager, or a folio. You just need to come in.

How to start03 / 08

Step 01

Walk in.

Or send work. Or get pointed to us by someone who's been. We don't have a casting call. The door is the application. Show us what you make. Tell us why this matters.

What happens next04 / 08

Step 02

A real conversation.

Thirty minutes at the studio. Not a panel. Not a test. Just an honest exchange. We look at the work, we ask why, we see how you talk about it. Most of the call is about who you are.

Step 03

The space

Studio, lighting, sets, edit bay, equipment. Free. No usage fees, no rentals, no quiet asks for favours. The space exists so you can make. Book it, use it, make work, hand it back to the next person.
