The principle

Plant what belongs, not what impresses.

Indigenous planting needs less water, feeds the insects that were already here, and holds the slope in a storm. A lawn is a decision to fight the climate you live in.

The truth

Ground recovers faster than people expect.

Stop the damage, put the right things back, and land that looked finished starts returning within a few seasons. It is the most hopeful fact we work with.

Who does it

Restoration is a crowd, not a hero.

One person with a spade is a nice photograph. Forty people on a slope for one Saturday changes a hillside. We do it the second way.
