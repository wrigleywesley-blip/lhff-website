Where it started02 / 07

The ground

It began as a strip of dead ground.

Compacted, sun-baked, walked over every day by children who did not know it could be anything else. Nobody planted there because nobody believed it would grow.

The work03 / 07

The work

Soil is made, not found.

Kitchen scraps, leaf litter, ash, patience. Months of feeding the ground before a single seed went in. The children did the turning. They learned that the boring part is the part that works.

Season one@lovehappinessfreedom
What grows04 / 07

What grows

Kale. Maize. Tomatoes. Beans.

Nothing exotic. Everything that stores well, feeds many, and can be replanted from its own seed next season. A garden that needs a shop is not a garden. It is a subscription.

The kitchen05 / 07

The kitchen

Twelve metres from bed to pot.

No truck. No cold chain. No middleman taking a cut of a child's lunch. The shortest supply chain in the world is the one you can walk.

The point06 / 07

The point

A fed child can learn. A hungry one survives.

Every education programme in the world is built on top of a meal. We stopped treating that as somebody else's department.
