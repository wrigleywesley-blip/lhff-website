One02 / 09

Small thing 01

Save one seed.

From the best tomato you eat this month. Dry it, label it, keep it. You have just opted out of buying that plant ever again.

Five minutes@lovehappinessfreedom
Two03 / 09

Small thing 02

Stop binning your peels.

A bucket with a lid is a composting system. Food scraps in a landfill produce methane. The same scraps in a bucket produce next season.

One bucket@lovehappinessfreedom
Three04 / 09

Small thing 03

Catch the rain.

One drum under one downpipe. It will not solve a drought. It will change how you think about a rainy afternoon, permanently.

One drum@lovehappinessfreedom
Four05 / 09

Small thing 04

Grow one thing you actually eat.

Not a decorative herb that dies on a windowsill. Something that ends up on a plate. The feedback loop is the whole point.

One pot@lovehappinessfreedom
Five06 / 09

Small thing 05

Fix it before you replace it.

A seam, a handle, a hinge. Repair used to be ordinary. It became a skill only because we stopped doing it.

One repair@lovehappinessfreedom
Six07 / 09

Small thing 06

Refuse the bag.

The single-use item you decline is the only one guaranteed never to reach a shoreline. Recycling is damage control. Refusing is prevention.

Every shop@lovehappinessfreedom
Seven08 / 09

Small thing 07

Plant a tree you will not sit under.

The oldest test of whether a person has understood any of this. Do something good that you will not be around to enjoy.

One tree@lovehappinessfreedom
