What we do

Catch it before it leaves.

Roofs are collection surfaces. Gutters are infrastructure. A tank beside a school building turns one rainy afternoon into weeks of garden water and nobody's borehole runs lower for it.

What we do05 / 07

And then

Keep it in the ground.

Mulch, shade, deep beds, watering at dusk instead of noon. Half of water saving is not using less. It is stopping the sun from taking what you already gave.
