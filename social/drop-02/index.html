Appendix

Notes & sources.

Every number is real

Statistics are drawn from peer-reviewed and official sources, including Stalmans et al. (PLOS One, 2019) and the Gorongosa Aerial Wildlife Count (2022) for the Gorongosa recovery; the Marine Mammal Protected Areas Task Force and Trotzuk et al. (2021) for dugongs; African Parks and Shapiro et al. (2018) for Bazaruto and mangroves; ANAC (2023) for protected-area coverage; Mozambique's National Forest Inventory (2018) for deforestation; and the Foundation's own Organic Seeds research (2025). Imagery is a blend of the Foundation's own library and cinematic stills generated for this drop. Motion graphics are built in Remotion and exported at 1080×1920.
