Programs & retreats

Six live programs. One operating philosophy.

Every program the Foundation runs is built on the same core practice: meet people where they are, do the actual work, leave them with something they can carry. No spectacle, no theatre, no filler.

01 / Retreats

Women's Empowerment Retreat

A five-day residential for twelve young women, held at Ilha Do Fogo on the Mozambican coast. Led by Cheney and the Foundation's resident practitioners.

Format
5 days, residential
Cohort
12 women, ages 18-29
Cadence
Quarterly
Cost
Scholarship + sliding scale

Pillars covered

  • → Purpose work
  • → Communication (spoken, written, embodied)
  • → Faith and prayer practice
  • → Redemption and forgiveness
  • → Community, sisterhood, sustained accountability

02 / Retreats

Corporate Retreat Programme

Commissioned retreats for leadership teams, commonly two to three days. We don't run corporate wellness theatre. We run the actual practice, adapted to a team's real situation.

Format
2-3 days, custom
Group size
Up to 20
Cadence
Quarterly or on-demand
Investment
On application

Who commissions us

Leadership teams in the middle of change: restructures, mergers, succession, a founder stepping back, a crisis of purpose. Companies who already know that offsites with ropes courses aren't the answer.

05 / Sustainability

Organic Seeds School Initiative

A school garden framework that teaches agronomy, nutrition, and regeneration from the soil up. Every partner school receives a seed library, a trained teacher, and a three-year curriculum.

06 / Advocacy

Model Protection Advocacy

Young women in fashion are protected by almost nothing. We're changing that. Research-led, policy-facing, movement-built. The Foundation publishes framework documents, supports affected women directly, and works alongside industry partners to push for real legal protection.
