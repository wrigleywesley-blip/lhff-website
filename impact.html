Case study 01

RESET Mozambique.

Rural Mozambican schools sit under particular pressure: female dropout, food insecurity, and a generation of teachers stretched beyond any reasonable capacity. RESET is our answer. A three-year school intervention built with local leadership, measured quarterly, and published annually.
