01 / What it is

Not a product. A practice, in software form.

Every wellness app on the market optimises for engagement. NAVID is built to optimise for your life outside the app. We measure it on whether you close the window and do something real.

Principle 01

Grounded in our work

Trained on the 7 Pillars curriculum, My Purpose, Triangle Empowerment, and the Foundation's research library. Not a generic LLM with a coat of paint.

Principle 02

Continuity, not conversation

For retreat graduates, NAVID remembers your pillar work. You come back to it, not to a cold start. It walks with you between retreats, not instead of them.

Principle 03

Private by default

Your conversations with NAVID belong to you. We don't sell them, analyse them for ad spend, or surface them in reporting. Full data policy below.

Principle 04

Knows its limits

NAVID is not a crisis service, a therapist, or a replacement for any of them. When a conversation needs a human, NAVID says so and offers real referrals.

02 / How it works

A presence, in three layers.

Public layer. Anyone can speak to NAVID. Answers about the Foundation, the programs, and the broader mission. Use it to navigate, not to confide.

Member layer. For retreat graduates and program participants. NAVID holds your pillar notes, your intentions, and the specific work you did with a practitioner. It picks up where the retreat left off.

Practitioner layer. For Foundation facilitators running retreats and school programs. NAVID drafts session outlines, surfaces research, and tracks cohort themes. It augments the practitioner, it doesn't replace them.

NAVID isn't meant to replace a conversation with a wise friend. It's meant to be the thing you open while you're waiting for that conversation.
