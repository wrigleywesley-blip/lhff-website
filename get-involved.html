Four doors. One mission.

The Foundation needs participants, partners, funders, and creators. Pick the door that fits where you actually are. None of them require you to be anyone you're not.

Door 01 / Participant

Apply for a retreat.

For young women between 18 and 29 ready to take five days to do real work on themselves, with practitioners, in community. Scholarships available on a sliding scale. Apply below or nominate someone who belongs here.

Door 02 / Organisation

Partner with the Foundation.

Schools, corporates, NGOs, foundations, research institutions. If your mission shares ground with ours, let's see what we can build that neither of us can build alone. We commit to named partnerships, published outcomes, and honest reporting.

For schools
7 Pillars curriculum integration
Bring the curriculum into your school day. Includes Organic Seeds garden, teacher residency, mentorship network.
For corporates
Corporate retreat commissioning
Two to three day custom retreat for leadership teams. Designed to your situation, not off a shelf.
For institutions
Co-funded research partnerships
Commission a research piece, support an existing one, or build a joint framework. All work published openly.
Open a partnership conversation
Door 03 / Funder

Fund the next chapter.

Door 04 / Creator

Join the Creative Studios.

Podcasters, filmmakers, photographers, wellness educators: you get free use of our Woodstock HQ studios, we get one piece of co-produced work per quarter. No fees, no exclusivity clauses, no awkward small print.
