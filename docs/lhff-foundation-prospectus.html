01 / The premise

A foundation built to scale a feeling.

The Love Happiness Freedom Foundation began as a single mural and grew into a body of work that crosses creative, spiritual, and conservation lines.

It is not a typical non-profit. There are no glossy reports, no rooms full of polished agendas. There are retreats, run quietly. A school in Mozambique that holds the curriculum as a living environment. A studio in Cape Town that opens its doors to young creatives who can't pay to learn. An AI companion that holds the work between sessions. And a body of conservation work along the Mozambican coast.

Behind all of it sits the same question: how do you scale a way of being? Not with marketing. With infrastructure. The seven pillars taught in every room. The same voice running through every interaction. The same care, whether you are in the villa at sunrise or reading this page.

"The work isn't a programme. It's a practice. The Foundation just builds the rooms it happens in."

— Cheney, Founder

02 / The three pillars

Three words. Twenty years of work.

The Foundation's name is also its method. Love. Happiness. Freedom. The order matters. Love is the door, happiness is the practice, freedom is what you build for the people who come after you.

Pillar 01
Love.
Love the earth. Love each other. Love the self that has been waiting.
Pillar 02
Happiness.
Not the absence of pain. The presence of meaning, built daily.
Pillar 03
Freedom.
The work you do for the people who come after you. Inner first. Outer follows.

Every retreat, every classroom at the school, every conversation NAVID holds runs on this triangle. It is the Foundation's grammar.

03 / The curriculum Seven pillars

03 / The seven pillars curriculum

The frame holding every retreat.

Inside the three big words sits a seven-part curriculum. The framework practitioners teach during the retreats, the framework the school holds for its children, the framework NAVID is trained on. Each pillar has its own sessions, its own readings, its own homework.

01
Purpose
Knowing why you're here. Personal, professional, spiritual. Most people skip the question.
02
Wellness
Sleep, breath, food, movement. The body is the room everything else happens in.
03
Communication
Spoken, written, embodied. Most conflict is communication, not content.
04
Meditation
Ten quiet minutes a day, every day, until the mind stops running you.
05
Discipline
The bridge between knowing and doing. Small commitments, kept consistently.
06
Faith
Not a religion. A relationship with what you can't see and still trust.
07
Redemption
Forgive yourself first. Forgive the people who taught you to be hard.

04 / The retreats

Three formats. One framework.

The Foundation runs three retreat formats at the Cape Town villa. Each one carries the same seven pillars, calibrated to a different cohort and a different rhythm. All are residential, practitioner-led, twelve guests at a time.

Format 01 · Women

Women's Empowerment · Empower your true inner self.

6 days12 women · 18–29QuarterlyScholarship + sliding scale

A six-day residential for young women ready to take a week and do real work on themselves. Built around purpose, communication, faith, and redemption. Led by Cheney and the Foundation's resident practitioners.

Format 02 · Corporate

Corporate Retreats · Leadership from the inside out.

4–6 days10–14 leadersBy arrangementTier pricing

Founders, executive teams, leadership cohorts. The retreat dropped onto strategy. Practical applications of the seven pillars to organisations: how teams talk, how decisions get made, how purpose holds at scale.

Format 03 · Spiritual

Spiritual Retreat · Sit with what's underneath.

6 days12 guests · mixedTwice yearlyBy application

The deepest of the three formats. For people ready to face what they have been carrying. Meditation, faith, redemption pillars run heavy. Slower pace, more silence, more held space. By application only.

04 / The retreats A retreat day

A day inside the work

Hour by hour.

Six days at the villa look like this. The shape stays the same. The teaching content shifts.

06:30
Sit before the day arrives.

Twenty minutes of stillness on the deck. No phones. First decision of the day is to stop running.

08:00
Yoga at One Flow.

Bodywork before the mind shows up. Local teachers, Cape Town studio.

10:00
A pillar in depth.

Group teaching at the villa. One of the seven pillars, unpacked. Real questions. No theatre.

13:00
Long-table lunch.

Food cooked at the villa. Conversation that goes where it wants to. The community starts to form here.

15:00
A long walk.

Mostly in silence. The morning's teaching gets to settle. The body keeps moving so the mind can sit still.

19:00
Fire circle.

An open round on the villa deck. People share what they want to. Most cohorts call this the centre of the week.

06 / The school How it teaches

Inside the school

Every detail teaches.

The fountain is the first thing the children walk past. A painted figure in the Foundation's seven colours, hands in prayer, water falling. The mural that started everything sits on the wall behind. Love, Happiness, and Freedom written above it in colour.

The architecture of the school carries the message before anyone says it. The work the children do inside the classrooms is shaped by the world they walk through to get there.

What gets taught

  • Core academics — built into a curriculum that runs alongside the seven pillars, not against them.
  • Emotional literacy — recognising and naming feelings before they become behaviours.
  • Land and water — conservation as a daily practice, not a unit. Gardening, recycling, marine literacy.
  • Music and movement — daily, taught by visiting artists from Cape Town.
  • Spiritual practice — non-denominational, age-appropriate, always optional.

08 / NAVID An AI companion

The technology

An AI that listens
differently.

NAVID is the Foundation's AI companion. He sits inside the app and on the website, available to retreat guests and Foundation members between sessions.

What he does

  • Greets visitors based on where they are in the journey.
  • Recognises patterns: anxiety, grief, relationships, work, decisions, imposter feelings.
  • Holds the Foundation's voice: two to four sentences, ends with a soft question.
  • Remembers between visits. What you said last time is still here next time.
  • Adjusts retreat-specific topics based on whether you're attending.

What he won't do

  • Replace a therapist.
  • Push the retreat.
  • Store anything off your device without consent.
  • Pretend to be human.

Access

NAVID is locked. Only retreat guests and Foundation members can talk to him. Access is granted via a code issued by the Foundation. Visitors without a code are pointed to the application form.

Built end-to-end by Divergence, Cape Town.

09 / The Foundation app

The work doesn't end when the retreat does.

The app holds the seven pillars in your pocket. Courses you can return to. A private journal. A practice tracker. NAVID, available between sessions. The community.

Five features, well

01
Home — your dashboard
What's next, what's been left undone, what's been done well. The day's work without the noise of a normal app.
02
Courses — the pillars, on repeat
Audio lessons, written practices, short videos. Each pillar broken into weeks. Built for a flight, a walk, the kitchen table.
03
Journal — write what's true
A private journal nobody sees. Daily prompts, weekly reflections. Encrypted on device.
04
Practice tracker — a streak that matters
Meditation, breathwork, movement, journaling. Quietly tracked. Not gamified.
05
NAVID, inside
Tap the corner. Ask anything. Trained on the Foundation's voice, available between sessions.

Built end-to-end by Divergence on React Native + Expo. iOS + Android. Available to retreat guests and Foundation members.

10 / Apply & get involved

Five doors.
Pick the right one.

There are five ways to step into the Foundation's work. Each one has its own application path. None of them require an agency, a referral, or a previous relationship with us.

Door 01 · For yourself

Apply for a retreat.

Women's Empowerment, Corporate, or Spiritual. Tell us which format, when, and why. Cohort applications open quarterly.

Door 02 · For your team

Bring a corporate cohort.

For founders, executive teams, leadership groups. Custom format, on-site at the villa, by arrangement.

Door 03 · For your work

Apply to Studio One.

Send a portfolio, have a conversation, get access to the studio. Free, ages 18–28, South African based.

Door 04 · For the mission

Donate or partner.

Fund a retreat scholarship. Fund the school. Partner on conservation work. Every contribution is acknowledged.

Door 05 · For everything else

Get in touch.

Press, partnerships, speaking, advisory. Leave your details on the contact form and we will come back within two working days.
